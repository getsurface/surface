How it works

No agent, no install, nothing touched on your server. We read only what it already hands out.

1

Paste your URL

Drop in any public address. We request your page the way a normal browser would.

2

We read the response

We send a few ordinary requests: your page over HTTPS, a plain-HTTP check and the certificate handshake. Then we read the replies.

3

Get plain fixes

Each finding comes with what it means and the one line to change. No jargon.
